    The rules from the data sheet
    • Usable height 600, 900 or 1,225 mm – cabinet height 875, 1,175 or 1,500 mm, footprint 800 × 790 mm (W × D).
    • Drawer heights 75, 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 250 and 300 mm. Together they fill the usable height exactly; drawers are always installed from smaller (top) to larger (bottom).
    • Up to 80 kg per drawer, optionally 160 kg for drawer heights of 150 mm or higher.
    • Partitions for 12, 16, 24 or 48 compartments, freely placed in the grid on a usable area of 602 × 602 mm. 48 compartments are available up to a drawer height of 175 mm.
    • The »smartCompartment« with 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 or 36 compartments is fitted onto the partitions of a mechanically locked drawer; 36 compartments only for 75 to 150 mm. Compartment depth = 19 mm less than the drawer height.
    • The mechanical cabinet has a central lock and any number of drawers. The electronic cabinet has a maximum of 20 drawers and needs a control system, for example table electronics, the »cockpit« or a TMS workstation.
    • Software for the electronic version: ZOLLER Smart Cabinets base package, ZOLLER TMS version 1.18.23 or higher with interface, Integration TMS.
